Bottle Boy: Request Data Deletion

Bottle Boy by Tasste of Life Studios has no user accounts. The only data stored on our servers is what you choose to save to the public top score board: the name you entered, your score, the number of customers served, the level you reached, the difficulty mode, whether you won, and when the score was saved.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ttuhoib@gmail.com with the subject Bottle Boy data deletion and include:

  • the name you used on the top score board
  • your score (if you remember it)
  • the approximate date you saved it

We will delete the matching scores from the top score board within 30 days and reply to confirm. If we can't find a matching score, we will ask you for more details.

What is deleted

All of the information listed above for the matching scores is permanently deleted. Any reports other players made about those names are deleted with them. We do not keep backups of deleted scores.

Data on your own device

The game also keeps a few settings only on your device (best score, difficulty, last name entered, sound setting). You can remove these yourself by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.
