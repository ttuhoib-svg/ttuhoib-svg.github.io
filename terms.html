Bottle Boy Board Rules

These rules apply when you save a name to the Bottle Boy top score board. The board is public: everyone who plays the game can see the names on it.

By saving a score you agree that:

  • You will not use names that are offensive, hateful, sexual, threatening or harassing, or that insult a real person or group.
  • You will not use someone else's real name without their permission, and you will not share personal information such as phone numbers or addresses.
  • You will not try to post fake scores or misuse the board.

Every name on the board has a report button (⚑). Reporting a name hides it from you right away, and names that are reported several times are hidden from everyone. We review reports and remove names and scores that break these rules, without notice.

How your name and score are stored is explained in the privacy policy.
