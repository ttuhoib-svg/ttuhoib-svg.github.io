Bottle Boy Privacy Policy

Bottle Boy is a game made by Tasste of Life Studios.

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The game saves two small settings only on your own device: your best score and whether sound is muted. This data never leaves your device, and you can remove it by clearing the app's data.

The game loads its pixel font from Google Fonts. Google may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the font is downloaded.

If you have questions, contact: ttuhoib@gmail.com
