Bottle Boy Privacy Policy

Bottle Boy is a game made by Tasste of Life Studios. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Top score board

At the end of a game you can choose to save your score to the public top score board. If you do, the game sends the following to our database:

  • the name or nickname you enter (up to 10 characters)
  • your score, the number of customers served, the level you reached, the difficulty mode, and whether you won
  • the time the score was saved

This information is shown publicly on the top score board to everyone who plays the game. If you don't want your real name to be public, use a nickname instead. Saving a score is optional: you can always choose SKIP.

The database is provided by Supabase and hosted in the European Union. When the game connects to it, Supabase also processes basic technical information such as your IP address, as any web service does. We do not use this information to identify you.

Scores are kept as long as the top score board exists. To have a score removed, contact us and tell us the name, score and approximate date.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few small settings only on your own device: your best score, your chosen difficulty, the last name you entered and whether sound is muted. This data never leaves your device, and you can remove it by clearing the app's data.

Fonts

The game loads its pixel font from Google Fonts. Google may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the font is downloaded.
